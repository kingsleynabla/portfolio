About Me

Designing with purpose.
Building with precision.

I'm Kingsley Ezekiel Nabla — a UI/UX Designer and Web Developer based in Ghana, passionate about creating digital experiences that are as beautiful as they are functional.

My work spans the full spectrum of digital product creation: from deep user research and wireframing to pixel-perfect visual design and front-end development. I believe great design is invisible — it gets out of the way and lets people do what they came to do.

I've designed and built real-world platforms across fintech, education, and community sectors — including the Ghana FinTech Awards platform, the Fanaka Education ecosystem, and several other digital products reaching thousands of users across West Africa.

How I Work

My design process

A disciplined workflow that turns ambiguous problems into polished products.

01

Discover

Research users, understand the problem space, identify constraints and opportunities.

02

Define

Synthesize findings into clear problem statements, user journeys, and solution criteria.

03

Design

Wireframes, high-fidelity prototypes, visual systems, and interaction design in Figma.

04

Build

Develop responsive, accessible, performant products using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

05

Improve

Test with real users, gather data, iterate and refine until the product truly works.
